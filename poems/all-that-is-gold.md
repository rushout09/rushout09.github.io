<h2 class="poem-title">All That Is Gold Does Not Glitter</h2>
<p class="poet">J. R. R. Tolkien, from <cite>The Fellowship of the Ring</cite></p>

<div class="poem">
<p>"All that is gold does not glitter,
Not all those who wander are lost;
The old that is strong does not wither,
Deep roots are not reached by the frost.</p>
<p>From the ashes a fire shall be woken,
A light from the shadows shall spring;
Renewed shall be blade that was broken,
The crownless again shall be king."</p>
</div>
